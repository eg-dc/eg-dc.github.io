Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what the “ExtremeGiveawaysDC” Discord application and its web panel (together, the “Service”) collect and how they use it. By adding or using the Service you agree to it. Contact: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.

1. Data we collect

Giveaway data (stored per server)

  • Giveaway details: prize, description, winner count, end time, channel and message IDs, ID of the member who created it, role IDs for requirements, bonus entries and winner roles, optional DM text.
  • Participant lists: the Discord user IDs of members who joined, and the winners and rerolls of ended giveaways.

Statistics (per server, per user ID): number of participations, wins and losses.

Server settings: audit log channel ID, ping role ID, minimum requirements (participations, wins, losses, account age, server days), and the list of blocked user IDs.

Recurring schedules: the same configuration as a giveaway plus interval, next run and run count.

Activity log: up to the last 200 entries per server, e.g. “User X created/edited/cancelled giveaway Y”, which may contain user mentions (IDs) and prize names. If you set an audit channel, entries are also posted there.

Web panel: when you sign in with Discord we request only the identify scope. We receive your user ID and display name, keep them in server memory for the session, and revoke the Discord access token right after login. A session cookie (gw_session, HttpOnly, up to 7 days) and a short login cookie (gw_state, 10 minutes) are set. Your browser’s local storage remembers which server you last selected. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers and do not keep web access logs.

2. Data we read but do not store

To check entry requirements and pick winners the bot reads, live from Discord: your roles, account creation date, server join date, display name and whether you are still a member. These are not saved. Names shown in participant lists are looked up at display time. A manager’s CSV export contains user ID, username and display name generated at export time and is not retained by us. The bot does not read or store message content.

3. How we use data

  • To run giveaways: accept entries, enforce requirements, draw and announce winners, assign winner roles and send prize DMs you were chosen to receive.
  • To show statistics, leaderboards and the web panel.
  • To keep the Service secure and fix abuse, for example blocking users or servers that abuse it.
  • To keep backups so data survives crashes (the most recent snapshots are kept on our server).

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles outside the giveaway features.

4. Sharing

Data is visible to the Discord server where you participate: members and managers of that server may see your name in participant lists and leaderboards, and managers can export lists. We do not share data with third parties except the infrastructure needed to operate the Service: Discord (the platform itself, governed by Discord’s Privacy Policy), a tunnel provider (zrok) that relays web panel traffic, and GitHub Pages which hosts this website and may log visitor IP addresses under GitHub’s policy. We may disclose data if required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Giveaway, statistics and settings data are kept while the bot is in your server so features keep working.
  • Sessions expire after 7 days or when you log out. The activity log keeps only the latest 200 entries per server.
  • You can ask us to delete your data, or a whole server’s data, at any time by contacting us (see below). Server owners can also ask us after removing the bot. We aim to respond within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights. We will need your Discord user ID to find your data. You can also see your own stats with /my_stats and leave giveaways with /leave_giveaway.

Our legal bases (where applicable) are performing the service you requested (joining a giveaway) and our legitimate interest in operating a secure giveaway service.

7. Security

Data is stored on our server with restricted access, the web panel uses HttpOnly cookies and CSRF protection, and permissions are checked live against Discord on every request. No system is perfectly secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in their country). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. Contact us if you think we have and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

The Service may be operated and data processed outside your country. By using it you understand your data may be transferred to and processed in other countries.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Continued use means you accept the update.

Privacy questions or deletion requests: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com
