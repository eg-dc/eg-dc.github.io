Everything a giveaway needs, nothing it doesn’t.

Built for community managers who want fair draws and less manual work.

Recurring giveaways
Start a fresh giveaway every day, week or month automatically, with a run limit if you want one.
Bonus entries per role
Reward boosters, supporters or veterans with extra chances. Every extra entry is shown on the giveaway.
Entry requirements
Require a minimum account age, server membership time, past participations or wins, or specific roles.
Winner roles & DMs
Give winners a role and send prize details by direct message automatically.
Fair rerolls
Reroll any ended giveaway. Previous winners are skipped while other eligible members remain.
Blacklist & audit log
Block abusers from entering and keep a log of every action in a channel of your choice.
Web panel
Sign in with Discord to join giveaways, see the leaderboard and, with Manage Server, create and edit giveaways in your browser.
Statistics & CSV export
Personal stats, a server leaderboard and participant lists as CSV.

Up and running in three steps

  1. Add the bot

    Click “Add to Discord” and pick your server.

  2. Run /giveaway

    Enter a prize, winner count and duration such as 1h30m or 2d.

  3. Let it run

    Members join with one button. The bot draws winners and announces them when time is up.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Adding the bot and all features are free.

Which permissions does the bot need?

View Channels, Send Messages and Embed Links. Manage Roles is only needed for winner-role rewards; use the invite with Manage Roles if you want that, and place the bot’s role above the reward role.

What data does the bot store?

Only what giveaways need: Discord IDs, participation counts and your server settings. No message content. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How does the web panel decide who can manage giveaways?

You sign in with Discord (identity only). Your rights are read live from the server: members can view and join, people with Manage Server or Administrator can manage.

Is the code public?

No. The bot is closed source.

Can’t see the slash commands?

Make sure the bot was added with the applications.commands scope (the invite link here includes it) and that your server’s Integrations settings allow the commands in that channel.
